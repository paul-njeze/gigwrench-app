# GigWrench Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: effective on posting.
Applies to: all users of the GigWrench platform.
Contact: support@gigwrench.app

This policy describes conduct that is not permitted on GigWrench. It supplements the Terms of Service. Violations may lead to the actions in the Enforcement and Account Actions Policy, including a warning, a temporary suspension, an indefinite suspension, or permanent removal.

## 1. Lawful use

Do not use the platform for any unlawful purpose or to facilitate unlawful activity. Comply with all laws, licensing requirements, and regulations that apply to you and to the services you provide or request.

## 2. Honesty and identity

Do not impersonate others, misrepresent your identity, qualifications, licensing, or affiliation, or provide false information during registration or identity verification. Do not create accounts to evade enforcement actions.

## 3. Safety

Do not engage in conduct that threatens the safety of any person, including threats, violence, harassment, stalking, or endangering others during or around a job. Follow safe practices appropriate to the work.

## 4. Respectful conduct

Do not harass, abuse, demean, or discriminate against others. Hate speech and targeted abuse are not permitted.

## 5. Fraud and financial abuse

Do not engage in fraud, deceptive pricing, unauthorized charges, payment scams, or money laundering. Do not manipulate ratings, reviews, or reputation signals, and do not submit false reports about other users.

## 6. Platform integrity

Do not tamper with, disable, falsify, or circumvent platform features, including location sharing during travel, identity verification, and safety mechanisms. Do not spoof, fake, or interfere with location or arrival detection.

## 7. Off-platform circumvention

Do not use the platform to find or solicit users and then deliberately move the transaction off-platform in order to evade applicable fees or the protections the platform provides.

## 8. Security

Do not probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the platform, breach security or authentication measures, access data not intended for you, or interfere with service to any user. Do not scrape, harvest, or collect other users' data without authorization.

## 9. Content

Do not post content that is illegal, infringing, sexually exploitative, harmful to minors, or that violates the privacy or rights of others. You are responsible for the content you submit.

## 10. Child safety

The platform is for adults. Any content or conduct that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors is strictly prohibited, will be acted on immediately, and may be reported to the appropriate authorities.

## 11. Reporting

If you encounter conduct that violates this policy, report it through the in-product reporting tools or by contacting support@gigwrench.app. Reports are reviewed by the Trust and Safety process described in the Enforcement and Account Actions Policy.

## 12. Consequences

Violations may result in a warning, a temporary suspension for a defined period, an indefinite suspension, or permanent removal, depending on severity and history, as described in the Enforcement and Account Actions Policy.
