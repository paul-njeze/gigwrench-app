# GigWrench Privacy Policy

Effective date: effective on posting.
Controller: GigWrench.
Contact: support@gigwrench.app

This Privacy Policy explains what information GigWrench collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

- Account information: name, email, phone number, role, language, and password credentials handled by our authentication provider.
- Identity verification: when required, identity information you submit is processed by a third-party verification provider. We receive the verification result and limited related data.
- Location information: while a Pro is traveling to a Customer for a job, we collect the Pro's live location to show the Customer the Pro's progress and arrival. Collection is limited to the active trip and stops on arrival.
- Job and usage information: jobs, bookings, messages, addresses associated with jobs, and activity on the platform.
- Payment information: where payments are offered, payment details are collected and processed by a third-party payment provider; we do not store full payment card numbers.
- Device and log information: standard technical data such as IP address and device information used to operate and secure the service.

## 2. How we use information

- To operate the platform, connect Pros and Customers, and manage jobs.
- To show live trip progress and arrival to the relevant Customer during an active trip.
- To verify identity where required, and to maintain safety and integrity.
- To send service messages, including booking, trip, account, and trust-and-safety notifications.
- To investigate reports and enforce our Terms and Acceptable Use Policy.
- To meet legal obligations and to protect users and the public.

## 3. Location data specifics

Live location is shared only with the Customer for the active job and only while the Pro is traveling to that job. The Pro consents before sharing begins, sharing is required only for the duration of that travel, and it ends automatically on arrival. Live location records are retained for up to ninety days and are then deleted or anonymized, unless a longer period is required for an open investigation or by law.

## 4. How we share information

- With the other party to a job, as needed to perform the job, including live trip progress to the Customer.
- With service providers that help us operate, such as hosting, messaging, identity verification, mapping, and payments, under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
- For legal and safety reasons, where we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect the safety of users or the public.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you have rights to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to the use of your information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, contact support@gigwrench.app. We will verify your request and respond within thirty days, and where a request is complex we may extend that period and will tell you if we do. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

## 6. Security

We maintain enterprise-grade security standards to protect your information, including access controls, server-side authorization, row-level data access limits, audit logging of administrative actions, and least-privilege administrative access. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

## 7. Data retention

We retain account information while your account is active and for up to twenty-four months after closure, unless a longer period is required by law. Job and transaction records are retained for up to seven years to meet tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, and legal obligations. Live location records follow the ninety-day rule above. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed for these purposes.

## 8. Children

The platform is for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it.

## 9. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where we transfer personal information across borders, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or an equivalent recognized mechanism, to protect it.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the updated version with a new effective date.

## 11. Contact

Questions or requests: support@gigwrench.app.
