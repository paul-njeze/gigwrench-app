# GigWrench Enforcement and Account Actions Policy

Effective date: effective on posting.
Contact: support@gigwrench.app

This policy explains how GigWrench reviews reported conduct and what actions we may take. It is written to match the mechanisms built into the platform.

## 1. How a review begins

A review may begin when a user submits a report about an account, or when our Trust and Safety process identifies a possible violation. When we open a review of an account, we notify the account holder that their account is under review. The notice is neutral and does not assume a finding. No action on your part is required at that stage unless we ask for information.

## 2. The decision

When a review concludes, we record one of the following outcomes and notify the account holder of the decision:

- Cleared: no violation found, the account remains active.
- Warning: a formal warning is recorded on the account.
- Suspended: access is suspended for a defined period or indefinitely.
- Disabled: the account is permanently removed.

## 3. Temporary and indefinite suspension

A suspension may be set for a defined length, for example one to several weeks or one to several months, or to a specific end date, or it may be indefinite. During a suspension you cannot sign in. A suspension with a defined end date is lifted automatically when that date passes, and we notify you that access has been restored. An indefinite suspension remains until we lift it.

## 4. Permanent removal

A disabled account is permanently removed from the platform and cannot sign in. Permanent removal is reserved for serious or repeated violations and is applied by senior administrators.

## 5. Notifications

We notify the account holder by email at the registered address when a review is opened, when a decision is made, and when a defined-period suspension ends. For temporary suspensions, the decision notice states the end date.

## 6. Appeals

If you believe an action was taken in error, you may request a review of the decision by contacting support@gigwrench.app within thirty days of the decision notice. We will acknowledge your appeal and aim to provide a final response within thirty days of receiving it. Appeals are reviewed by a senior administrator who was not the original decision-maker where reasonably possible.

## 7. Records and accountability

We keep an internal record of enforcement actions, including who acted, when, and the reason, so that decisions are traceable and reviewable. Access to enforcement tools is limited to authorized administrators on a least-privilege basis, and customer-care staff have a reduced level of access that does not permit permanent removal.

## 8. Safety and security

We maintain enterprise-grade security standards to protect accounts and data. Where conduct presents a risk to the safety of users or the public, or involves the safety of minors, we may act immediately and may notify the appropriate authorities.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version, with its effective date, governs.
